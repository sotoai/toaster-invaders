Backrooms level credits

The environments in Toaster Invaders adapt these articles from The Backrooms Wikidot. The pixel artwork is newly drawn in code; no photographs or music from the wiki are used.

  • Level 0 — Threshold: DivineAtlas, DrAkimoto, and RobertGoerman; earlier adaptation by Deveyerr, based on the original anonymous concept. Yellow wallpaper, damp carpet, segmented interiors, and fluorescent lighting.
  • Level 1 — Habitable Zone: Praetor3005 and DivineAtlas; earlier versions by u/ThePizzaEater1000 and etoisle, original concept by EnderMitten. Concrete warehouse spaces, support pillars, and supply crates.
  • Level 2 — Abandoned Utility Halls: Boring Talking X_X; earlier versions by u/M654z and 1000dumplings. Cramped industrial passages, pipes, machinery, and scattered debris.
  • Level 3 — Electrical Station: Natedagreat563; original by u/M654z. Brick corridors, electrical cabinets, cables, and larger generator spaces.
  • Level 37 — Sublimity: egglord. White ceramic tiles, blue-green water, submerged steps, pillars, and irregular pool spaces.
  • Level Fun — party rooms: based on the Level Fun description in The Manila Room by Hikarinu (original by Anonymous Fandom User). Party decorations inspire the environment; the toaster party creatures are original. The separate Wikidot Level Fun page currently redirects, so this adaptation follows the cited overview.
  • Level 35 — An Empty Car Park: thelastpizzaroll. Concrete parking spaces and warm, green, and blue lighting inspire this game environment. The source describes a finite six-floor structure; our scrolling map is a simplified single-floor adaptation.

These environment adaptations are available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0, matching the wiki text license. Changes include simplified top-down layouts, procedural scenery, and game objectives.

This is a Toaster Invaders interpretation, not a canonical recreation. Toaster monsters, combat tasks, and the repeating 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 37 → Fun → 35 route are original gameplay additions. In particular, the wiki describes Level 37 as devoid of entities.
